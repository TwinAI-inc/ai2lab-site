The Science of Story: Flow State & Learning

Narrative Transportation Theory

When we hear a compelling story, we become "transported" - we lose ourselves in the narrative world, experiencing focused attention, emotional engagement, and mental imagery. This psychological state makes us more open to new ideas and less critical of the message.

Green & Brock (2000) - Wikipedia: Transportation Theory
