Which piece of work is this for?

One form per student, per piece of work. Everyone on the team fills in their own, including members of teams that are not presenting.

What you are confirming

The first four are about the work. The one in red is about you, and ticking it says you have already spoken to me if a teammate was not pulling their weight. If any of the five is not true, do not tick it: say what happened in the next step and come and talk to me.

What you did

This is the part that is actually read. Mark the areas you worked on, then say in your own words what you contributed. Be specific enough that a teammate reading it would recognise it as true.
Two or three sentences. Name the parts you built, not the areas you helped with.

Check it, then sign

Everything you are signing is on the right. Sign on the left, then download the form and hand it in.
