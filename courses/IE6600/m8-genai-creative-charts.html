Before you believe it
Four checks the model will not do for you
All four of these render perfectly and report no error, which is exactly why they have to be checked by hand.
Does the channel carry the numberArea, angle or length has to move with the value. Equal shapes for unequal numbers means the model ignored your measure.
Do the parts add upIf a breakdown totals more than its parent, a row is counted twice. This renders perfectly and reports no error.
Is the colour scale the right kindSequential on a total, diverging on a rate, categorical on names. A rainbow on an ordered measure is the most common thing to come back.
Does the title state a findingThis data has one in it: the largest region grows the slowest and the smallest grows the fastest. Ask again until the title says something like that.
