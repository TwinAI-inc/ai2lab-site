How this works

Sutto starts bottom left and wants the flag. Every step costs him a little, the trap costs him a lot, and he has no map. He only finds the good route by trying routes and remembering how they went.

  • Move him with the arrow keys, or click a neighbouring square.
  • Auto-learn plays episodes for you so you can watch instead.
  • Show policy draws his current best move in every square.
  • The table on the right only changes when an episode ends.
