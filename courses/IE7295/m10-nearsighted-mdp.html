The Nearsighted MDP Problem

Why discount factors are problematic for continuing tasks

Problem Setup

Structure: Two circular paths (rings) that share state S. From S, the agent chooses LEFT or RIGHT.

Left Ring: Reward +1 at the top (first transition leaving S, 1 step away)

Right Ring: Reward +2 at the bottom (last transition returning to S, 4 steps away)

Question: Intuitively, +2 > +1, so RIGHT is better. But does the discount factor γ change this?

Critical Threshold: γ 0.841 required for RIGHT to be preferred.

With 5 states, γ 0.841. As rings grow larger, γ must approach 1.0, causing numerical instability—this is why discount formulation is problematic for large continuing tasks.
