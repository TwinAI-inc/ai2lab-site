How to Play & Learn

Your Mission

You're facing a classic problem in decision-making: explore vs exploit. Each arm is a slot machine with hidden reward patterns. Your goal? Maximize total rewards!

Phase 1: Exploration

During exploration, you're learning about each arm's behavior. Pull different arms to discover:

• Which arms give consistently high rewards

• Which arms are unpredictable (high variance)

• The average reward you can expect from each

Phase 2: Exploitation

Now it's time to earn! Use your knowledge from exploration to maximize rewards.

Should you always pull the best arm? Or hedge your bets? The choice is yours!

Pro Tips

• Hover over the ℹ button to see the true distributions

• Watch the mini charts to track your performance over time

• The slot machine animation shows your reward - middle box is your score!
