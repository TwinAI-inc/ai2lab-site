Logistics & fine print

Booking lead times, holiday flags, partner-university coordination, and the practical math behind running this program.

Course (TBD)

Two 4-credit courses, 8 credits total. Direction: 1000-level cross-major intro open to all departments — engineering, business, sciences, humanities. Main course (IE 4510) runs every weekday morning except day-trip and corporate-visit days. Final code TBD with NEU course catalog office.

Cohort

Min 10 / max 16 students. GPA ≥ 3.0. 1 Program Assistant. Open to all majors and undergraduate years (3+ recommended).

Host institution

Università degli Studi di Parma — Department of Engineering & Architecture (DIA). Classroom on the Campus of Science & Technology (4 km south of city centre).

Transit

  • Trenitalia + Italo high-speed for day trips and Milan move
  • FrecciaYOUNG (under-30) for cohort discounts
  • Local bus + walking in Parma centro

Holiday alert

Wed Jun 2, 2027 = Festa della Repubblica. Factories closed (Ferrari, Lamborghini, Maserati, Barilla all dark). Mantua day trip slotted on this date — UNESCO city, museums and historic sites stay open.

Housing

Hotel in Parma for arrival weekend (May 27-30). UniPR-coordinated student housing in Parma weeks 1–3. Hotel/student residence in Milan for week 4 (POLIMI Bovisa or city centre, TBD with GSoM office).

Health & safety

NEU GEO insurance enrollment + Italy entry requirements briefing during NEU pre-departure orientation. Local emergency contacts in PA-curated handbook.
