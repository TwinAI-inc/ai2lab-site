Module 11 · Probabilistic Risk Analysis

Monte Carlo NPV Simulator

Deterministic NPV asks one question and gives one number. Reality has dozens of unknowns. Pick a distribution for each uncertain input, roll the dice 10,000 times, and look at the whole landscape of futures — not just the average.

Why Monte Carlo?  ·  the math in one minute
Trial NPV

For each trial \(k = 1, 2, \ldots, K\) we draw one sample from each distribution and compute:

\( \mathrm{NPV}_k = -C_0 + \displaystyle\sum_{t=1}^{N_k} \frac{A_k - O_k}{(1+i)^t} + \frac{S_k}{(1+i)^{N_k}} \)

where \(A_k\) is annual benefit, \(O_k\) is annual operating cost, \(N_k\) is project life, \(S_k\) is salvage, all drawn from their distributions on this trial. Across \(K\) trials we then look at the distribution of \(\mathrm{NPV}_k\) — its mean, its spread, its probability of being negative.
