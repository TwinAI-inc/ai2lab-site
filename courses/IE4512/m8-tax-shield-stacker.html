Module 8 · After-Tax Economic Analysis

Three deductions, stacked on one asset

The tax code lets you accelerate write-offs three ways on the same purchase. Stack them in the right order and you can expense most of an asset in its very first year — pulling the tax shield forward to where it is worth the most.

1 · §179 expensing
Immediately expense the asset up to a yearly cap ($1.16M in 2024). It comes off the top, before anything else.
2 · Bonus depreciation
A percentage of whatever basis is left, taken in year 1. But it is phasing out: 100% in 2022 down to 0% by 2027.
3 · Regular MACRS
The normal accelerated schedule applies to whatever basis the first two layers leave behind, spread over the asset's class life.
Order matters
§179 first, then bonus on the remainder, then MACRS on the rest. Each layer feeds the next — that is the stack.
Stacking can move a huge share of the deduction into year 1, where the tax shield is worth the most. But as bonus depreciation phases down toward zero, that front-loaded advantage shrinks. This tool shows the extra present worth the stack buys over plain MACRS — and how it fades by placed-in-service year.
