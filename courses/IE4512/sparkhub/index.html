Spring 2026 · Top Three Final Projects

Three teams. Three industries. One hard question: will it pay?

Every team built three interactive web tools to analyze their project — a break even calculator, a sensitivity slider lab, and a Monte Carlo simulator. These are the top three final projects from the Spring 2026 EMGT 6225 cohort, hosted here so future students can see what their own capstone could look like. Each tool is fully interactive in your browser. No login. No setup.

Group 9 · Section 1

Coffee Shop Expansion

A neighborhood coffee shop weighing a new location. Daily traffic, ticket size, and rent are uncertain — the team built three tools to ask whether the second store would break even, what drives the answer, and how much risk is involved.

Group 7 · Section 1

Continuous Glucose Monitor

A medical device team modeled the economics of a new continuous glucose monitor for the U.S. diabetic population. Adoption curve, reimbursement, and unit cost all uncertain — the three tools walk through the case from breakeven to full Monte Carlo.
