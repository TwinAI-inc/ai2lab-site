Module 8 · After-Tax Economic Analysis

Before tax tells half the story

A before-tax NPV pretends the tax collector does not exist. Bring in the corporate income tax and one quiet hero — depreciation — and the project's real number appears.

Tax is real cash out
Every year you hand the IRS the tax rate times your taxable income. After-tax cash flow sits below before-tax, year after year.
Depreciation shields it
Depreciation costs no cash, yet it lowers taxable income. Each dollar of depreciation saves you T dollars of tax — the depreciation tax shield.
MACRS front-loads
Accelerated schedules push the shield into the early years, where the time value of money makes each saved dollar worth more.
The shield has a price tag
Discount every year's shield back to today and you get its present worth — what depreciation is actually worth to this project.
Watch a healthy before-tax NPV shrink once the tax bites — then see how much the depreciation shield hands back. The gap between the two curves is the cost of taxes; the shield is your rebate. Change the MACRS class and the tax rate and watch both move.
