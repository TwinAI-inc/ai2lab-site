Insight

All four plans pay back the same loan, but their cash flow timing differs. The borrower's PW depends on the lender's rate (which sets the payments) and the borrower's TVOM (which discounts them).

The four curves cross at zero when Lender's Rate = Borrower's TVOM. There, every plan is equivalent — the borrower neither gains nor loses in PW.

To the left (Lender's Rate < TVOM): borrower benefits, and deferring payments amplifies the win. Preference: 4 ≻ 1 ≻ 3 ≻ 2.

To the right (Lender's Rate > TVOM): borrower loses, and deferring deepens the loss. Preference flips: 2 ≻ 3 ≻ 1 ≻ 4.
