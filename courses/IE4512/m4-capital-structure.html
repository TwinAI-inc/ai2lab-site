Why don't firms just lever up to 100% debt?

Cheap debt + tax shield should always lower the cost of capital, right? Up to a point — yes. Past that point, lenders and equity holders both demand more (default risk + financial distress), and WACC starts climbing again. The result is a U-shaped curve with an optimum somewhere in the middle.
