Why the schedule has n+1 years
n + 1
A 5-year MACRS property recovers over 6 calendar years. Year 1 = 50%, years 2 through 5 = full, year 6 = the remaining 50%. The half-year convention is what creates that extra year.

Why does this exist?

The IRS could ask you the exact day you placed each asset in service. They don't. The half-year convention is administrative simplification: it lets a small business buy a $40,000 truck in December and still claim half a year of depreciation, while the bigger firm that bought the same truck in March gets the same fraction. Mid-quarter is a backstop when too much of your basis (>40%) lands in Q4 — Congress's way of saying you cannot game the convention by waiting until year-end to buy everything.

Real estate gets mid-month because the dollar values are large enough that month-by-month precision starts to matter, but day-by-day still doesn't.
