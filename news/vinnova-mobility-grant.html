Prof. Dehghani Awarded Vinnova International Mobility Grant

October 2026 Vinnova, Sweden's Innovation Agency With Chalmers University of Technology

Prof. Mohammad Dehghani has been awarded a 2026 International Mobility grant from Vinnova, Sweden's national innovation agency. The grant establishes a funded research exchange between Prof. Dehghani and the production systems group at Chalmers University of Technology in Gothenburg, Sweden.

Project at a Glance

Project Goal
Expand joint research on digital twins and simulation based decision making for production systems.
Partnership
A funded research exchange between Northeastern University and Chalmers University of Technology in Gothenburg, Sweden.
Project Period
Starting November 2026, with a research exchange and collaborative work through 2027.
Expected Outcomes
Joint publications and follow-on research proposals building on the collaboration.
