Prof. Dehghani Awarded Vinnova International Mobility Grant

October 2026  |  Vinnova, Sweden's Innovation Agency  |  With Chalmers University of Technology, Gothenburg

Prof. Mohammad Dehghani has been awarded a 2026 International Mobility grant from Vinnova, Sweden's national innovation agency, establishing a funded research exchange with the production systems group at Chalmers University of Technology. The project is funded through Vinnova's International Mobility programme, with a focus on industrial digital twins.

Project Goal
Expand joint research on digital twins and simulation based decision making for production systems.
Partnership
A funded research exchange between Northeastern University and Chalmers University of Technology, Sweden.
